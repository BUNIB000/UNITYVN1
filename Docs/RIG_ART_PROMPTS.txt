RIG-READY ART PROMPTS (v2, built for smooth animation)
============================================================
WHAT WAS WRONG BEFORE
The old parts sheets drew every piece separately, so pieces did not match each other (different scale, shading and cut ends) and the joins showed. This time the character is drawn ONCE, as one complete figure in a rig-friendly pose. I cut the pieces out of that single painting (so they always match), and a second image gives the faces.

THE PLAN: 2 IMAGES PER CHARACTER
  1. FIGURE   one full-body picture in an "A-pose" (arms and legs slightly apart so every limb can be cut cleanly)
  2. FACES    one sheet of 12 heads (expressions, talking mouth, blink), made from the figure as a reference
That is 6 images for all three characters. 

START WITH MAYA ONLY (2 images). Look at the result before paying for the other four. If Maya's figure looks good, send me the two images and I will build the new cutter and show you the animation before you generate anything else.

CREDIT-SAVING TIPS
- Generate the FIGURE first. Then, in the SAME chat, upload or keep it as the reference and send the FACES prompt. This keeps the face, skin and hair identical.
- For Player and Leo, stay in the same chat and say: "Same style and framing. New character:" then paste the character line from their prompt.
- Do not ask for upscales or retries of minor flaws. Hands that are a bit odd are fine; I can redraw the hand pose with the engine. Only retry if the pose is wrong (arms touching the body, legs crossed, cropped feet).
- Use the largest standard size your tool gives. Portrait for the figure, landscape for the faces.

WHAT MAKES THE FIGURE "RIG-READY" (already built into the prompts)
- Plain flat green background, so cutting is clean.
- Arms held about 35 degrees away from the body, legs about shoulder-width apart, so no limb overlaps another.
- Hair kept off the shoulders and neck, no scarves, no long flowing clothes.
- Flat, even lighting with no cast shadows, so pieces can be moved without bad shading.
- Head-to-toe in frame, feet fully visible.

WHAT I DO WITH THEM
============================================================
Figure: I cut head-less body into torso/pelvis, upper arms, forearms with hands, and legs, with overlap at every joint, then bend the elbows, knees and waist smoothly (mesh-style) instead of rotating stiff tubes. Faces: swapped in for expressions, talking and blinking, with the neck blended into the body. Hand poses (point, fist, wave) are an optional extra later, only if you want them.
If you would rather skip the green background, plain white works too: change "#00FF00" to "white" in every prompt.
